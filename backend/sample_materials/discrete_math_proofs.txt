Discrete Mathematics: Propositional Logic & Graph Theory

1. Propositional Logic:
   - Proposition: A statement that is either True or False.
   - Tautology: A compound proposition that is always True.
   - Contradiction: A proposition that is always False.

2. Mathematical Induction Proof:
   - Base step: Prove P(1) is true.
   - Inductive step: Assume P(k) is true, prove P(k+1) is true.

3. Graph Theory:
   - Eulerian Graph: Contains a closed trail containing all edges of a graph.
   - Hamiltonian Cycle: Graph cycle that visits each vertex exactly once.
   - Pigeonhole Principle: If n items are put into m containers, with n > m, at least one container must contain more than one item.
