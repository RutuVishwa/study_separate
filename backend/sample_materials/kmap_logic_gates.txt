LDM: Logic Design and Microprocessor Concepts

1. Boolean Algebra & Logic Gates:
   - AND Gate: Output is HIGH only if all inputs are HIGH.
   - OR Gate: Output is HIGH if any input is HIGH.
   - NOT Gate: Inverts the logic level.
   - NAND / NOR: Universal Logic Gates.

2. Karnaugh Maps (K-Maps):
   - Used to simplify Boolean algebraic expressions without lengthy algebraic calculations.
   - 4-variable K-map contains 16 cells representing minterms.

3. Flip-Flops and Registers:
   - SR, JK, D, and T Flip-Flops store 1 bit of digital state.
   - Microprocessor 8086 registers: AX, BX, CX, DX general purpose registers.
